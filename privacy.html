Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026. This policy covers the Cut & Weld app for iPhone and iPad.

No data collected

Cut & Weld does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to a server.

Your media stays on your device

All editing, speech recognition, AI features and video export happen on your device. Your photos, videos, audio and projects are never uploaded. They leave your device only when you choose to share an exported video.

Permissions

  • Photos: to import the videos and photos you pick, and to save exported videos to your library.
  • Speech Recognition: to create captions. Recognition runs on device only.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
