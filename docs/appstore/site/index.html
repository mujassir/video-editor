Common questions

  • Do my videos get uploaded? No. Editing, captions, AI features and export all run on your device. There's no account and no server.
  • Captions say speech recognition isn't available. Captions use on-device speech recognition. Allow Speech Recognition for Cut & Weld in Settings, and make sure your language supports on-device recognition.
  • Where are exported videos saved? Tap Save to Photos after export, or Share to send the video to another app.
  • Is there a watermark? No, unless you add your own.
